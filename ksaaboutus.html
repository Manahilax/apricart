About Apricart

Apricart was built with a simple vision: to make quality food and everyday staples more accessible to baqalas and neighbourhood retailers across Riyadh.

“

Our ambition is to combine the reliability of traditional wholesale with the convenience of technology, making everyday sourcing simpler, more accessible and more cost-effective for retailers.

Wholesale, made simpler

We understand that running a store means constantly balancing product availability, changing prices and multiple suppliers. Apricart makes wholesale simpler by bringing essential food products together at competitive prices, backed by dependable supply, dedicated sales support and convenient doorstep delivery.

Built on long-term relationships

Our focus is on building long-term relationships with retailers and helping them keep their shelves stocked with the products their customers need, while managing costs and protecting margins.
