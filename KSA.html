About Apricart KSA

We make wholesale simpler for everyday retail

Apricart was built with a simple vision: to make quality food and everyday staples more accessible to baqalas and neighbourhood retailers across Saudi Arabia.

Running a store means balancing product availability, changing prices and multiple suppliers. We bring essential food products together at competitive prices, backed by dependable supply and doorstep delivery.

Our focus is on long-term relationships: helping retailers keep their shelves stocked while managing costs and protecting margins.
