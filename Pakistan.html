Before you ask

Coverage, terms and onboarding.

The questions procurement and finance teams ask us most before moving their supplies to Apricart.

Where do you deliver?

Confirm coverage — cities served, lead times for main cities and for remote sites.

Is there a minimum order or contract?

Confirm minimum order value, contract term and whether a pilot at one location is possible.

What are the payment terms?

Confirm credit terms, invoicing cycle, filer status and tax documentation provided.

How long does onboarding take?

Confirm typical time from agreement to first delivery, and what you need from the client.
