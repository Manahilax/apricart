The questions finance teams ask us most before moving their supplies to Apricart.

Where do you deliver?

Apricart provides nationwide delivery across Pakistan.
We operate our own warehousing and delivery capabilities in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad/Rawalpindi, supported by trusted logistics partners for deliveries across the rest of the country.
Today, we serve client branches from major commercial centres to locations across upper and lower Pakistan, enabling businesses to manage procurement centrally while delivering directly to individual branches and offices nationwide.

What categories can we procure through Apricart?

Apricart supports multiple procurement categories, from everyday office and operational supplies to business-specific requirements. This allows organisations to consolidate purchasing across categories while maintaining centralised pricing, approvals and visibility.

How does invoicing work across multiple locations?

Apricart simplifies invoicing across branches, locations and procurement categories through centralised, GST-compliant billing. This gives finance teams fewer invoices to manage, easier reconciliation and clearer visibility of organisation-wide spend.

What are your payment terms?

Payment terms are agreed centrally with each client, including the applicable credit period and pricing structure. Once agreed, these terms apply across authorised locations, allowing branches to order directly while billing and payment management remain centralised.
